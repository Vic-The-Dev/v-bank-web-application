About V-Bank

Welcome to V-Bank, a modern banking application designed to provide users with a simple, secure, and convenient way to manage their finances.

V-Bank allows users to create accounts, log in securely, manage their account balances, make deposits, withdraw funds, transfer money, and keep track of their transaction history.

Built with Python, Flask, and PostgreSQL, V-Bank focuses on reliable financial operations, secure authentication, efficient account management, and consistent transaction processing.
